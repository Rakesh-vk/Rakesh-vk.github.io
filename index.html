01

Asynchronous notifications

OrderService publishes an OrderCreatedEvent to order.created. NotificationService consumes it independently, so email delivery is decoupled from the synchronous checkout path. Consumption is deduplicated on (eventId, channel).

KafkaChoreographyIdempotency
02

Failure isolation

OrderService protects ProductService and PaymentService REST calls with Resilience4j circuit breakers. Connect/read timeouts and dedicated fallbacks turn downstream outages into explicit domain-level failures instead of hanging the whole request.

Resilience4jRestClientTimeouts
03

Defensive service boundaries

Each service owns its persistence model. JWTs are validated independently at service boundaries, and controllers expose DTOs instead of JPA entities. Validation and global exception handlers keep API errors consistent.
